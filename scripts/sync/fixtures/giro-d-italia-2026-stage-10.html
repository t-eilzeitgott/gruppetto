Story of the day

Filippo Ganna dominated the individual time trial, equalling Eddy Merckx's record of seven Giro ITT wins. He averaged 54.9 km/h, finishing nearly two minutes clear of teammate Thymen Arensman, with Rémi Cavagna third. In the GC, Afonso Eulálio survived to keep the maglia rosa, but his lead over Jonas Vingegaard was slashed to just 27 seconds.
