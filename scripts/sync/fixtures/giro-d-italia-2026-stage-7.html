Story of the day

This stage featured an four-man breakaway including Jonathan Milan, the final escapees were caught on the slopes of Blockhaus. With more than five kilometers remaining, Jonas Vingegaard launched a decisive attack. Giulio Pellizzari briefly followed before fading, while Felix Gall finished second and Jai Hindley caught Pellizzari late on. Pink jersey holder Eulalio lost nearly three minutes but retained the overall lead.
